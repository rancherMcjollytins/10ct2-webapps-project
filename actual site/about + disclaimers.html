Charity 4 Dummies

About Us

Charity 4 Dummies is a free tool that assists with the basic indexing, locating and contacting of charities, non-profits, volunteering and other similar organisations. inspired by sites like the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC). Please read the disclaimer below.

Disclaimer

This site was made as part of a school project, and should not be used, as information and features may be outdated. For proper information on charities (in Australia), use official government sites run by commissions like the ACNC and ASIC.
